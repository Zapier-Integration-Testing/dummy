National Email Week: Declutter and Secure Your Inbox
During National Email Week (June 9–15, 2025), experts recommend cleaning up your inbox, using folders and labels, unsubscribing from unnecessary lists, and archiving old messages. For security, enable multi-factor authentication, use email encryption, and participate in phishing awareness training.
Why this matters: Over 90% of cyberattacks start with phishing emails. Keeping your inbox organized and secure reduces risk, improves productivity, and helps protect your business from data breaches and compliance failures.
